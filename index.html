Seen a shark? Tell us.

Divers, dive masters, and fishers see more sharks than any scientist. Every report you make helps build a picture of where sharks live around the UK and Ireland, and how that is changing.

You do not need to be an expert. If you are not sure what you saw, say so, and add a photo if you can.

Why report "no sharks"?

If we only hear about sightings, the data shows where people go diving, not where sharks live. A record of a dive with no sharks is just as useful to a scientist.

Works at sea

Once you have opened this site, it works without a signal. Reports are saved on your phone. You can add it to your home screen from your browser's menu.

Who uses the data?

Reports are shared with the Biology Department at King's College School, Wimbledon, and with shark conservation charities. Exact locations are not published, to protect vulnerable species.
